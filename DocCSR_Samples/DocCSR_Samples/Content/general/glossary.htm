Glossary

NOTE To help you find your desired term, search by first letter on the On this page left menu, then browse the list of terms for the selected letter.

The glossary contains a directory of terms used in the application and the context in which they are used.

A

  • Action

  • Actual

  • Adjustment

  • Alerts

  • Amortization period

  • Average of previous periods (extrapolation method)

  • Average of previous years (extrapolation method)

  • Audit trail

Action

Last level of strategy deployment.

For example, a company implements a strategy to improve its CSR performance. As part of this strategy, the company will consider various stakes, such as environmental, social, and governance stakes. For each stake, the company will set targets. For the environmental stake, the company will set two targets level 1: one to reduce air pollution, the other to reduce water pollution. For each target, one or more targets level 2 are set: reducing GHG emissions, reducing the consumption of plant protection products. To achieve these targets level 1 and 2, the company decides to implement several actions, such as installing solar panels or replacing one product with another.

Actual

Actual quantitative results of a company's operations that have already occurred.

Actual results can be raw data collected or the aggregation of multiple collected data.

Adjustment

Request to modify data entered in a campaign that has been closed.

Alerts

Attached to indicators or actions, alerts inform you that there is important information to know regarding your indicator or action.

Amortization period

Accounting life of an asset whose emissions are calculated.

Average of previous periods (extrapolation method)

Only for half-yearly, quarterly and monthly campaigns.

The indicator value is calculated based on the average of the previous period values.

Average of previous years (extrapolation method)

For yearly campaigns only.

The indicator value is calculated based on the average of previous years' values.

Audit trail

Make it possible to follow up all the actions performed on a CSR Insight item (indicators, targets, users).

C

  • Carbon footprint

  • Chapter

  • Consistency check

  • Consolidation rate

  • Control modes

  • Currency

Carbon footprint

Module, integrated into the CSR Insight application, which helps you calculate the greenhouse gas emissions of an activity, product and/or entity.

Chapter

First classification level for indicators. Indicators defined in the CSR Insight application are divided into chapters, themes, and sections, by nesting:

  • Each chapter contains one or multiple themes.

  • Each theme contains one or multiple sections.

  • Each section contains one or multiple indicators.

Consistency check

Consists of defining the value of an indicator in relation to another entered value: for another period/year, for another indicator in the same period/year, and so on.

Besides, you can enable conditional display of indicators by setting consistency checks.

Consolidation rate

The consolidation rate (expressed as a percentage) reflects the level of ownership and control of entities within a group. In terms of financial and non-financial reporting, the consolidation rate is used to calculate the proportion of accounts to be integrated and the equivalence share.

Control modes

Financial control and operational control, which are two management modes, allow you to determine the organizational scope:

  • Financial control: 100% of the equipment and facilities on which the legal person exercises financial control are included in the organizational scope.

  • Operational control: 100% of the equipment and facilities on which the legal person exercises operational control, that is, it operates, are included in the organizational scope.

Currency

Unit associated to data of monetary indicators.

D

  • Dashboard

  • Data collection campaign

  • Data extrapolation

Dashboard

Performance monitoring and management tool that allows you to obtain an overview for an indicator or for an entity.

Data collection campaign

Item on which you can collect data for indicators, entities and over a given period, for reporting and sustainable development strategy purposes.

Data extrapolation

Can be used during data collection when data is not known but can be inferred using an extrapolation method:

  • Previous year

  • Average of previous years

  • Weighting

  • Estimation

  • Same period of the last year

  • Average of previous periods

  • Previous period

  • Weight of a reference indicator over a period of your choice

 

For more information, see the definition of each extrapolation method.

E

  • Emission factor

  • Emission reporting formats / Versions

  • Entity

  • Entity type

  • Estimation (extrapolation method)

Emission

Also called a greenhouse gas emissions, emissions are gases that are naturally present in the atmosphere and help intercept infrared radiation from the sun, such as for example the carbon dioxide (CO2). In the CSR Insight application, the emissions of an organization, an activity, a product are calculated by gas, then added together to give the final result in CO2e.

 

CO2e is a unit of measurement used to compare emissions of various greenhouse gases based on their global warming potential (GWP), by converting the amounts of the various gases emitted into the equivalent amount of carbon dioxide with the same global warming potential.

Emission factor

Coefficient that allows to convert activity data into emission in CO2e. Classified into a category and sub-category.

Emission reporting formats / Versions

Regulations on which the carbon footprint must be based: BEGES V4, BEGES V5, or GHG protocol.

Entity

Item from which your data comes. In the CSR Insight application, each indicator is deployed on at least one entity within a campaign.

 

Entities are hierarchized by generation level with parent and child entities:

  • The entity with the level 1 generation is the parent of all child entities. This entity is by default displayed at the top of the nested grid, that is the first line.

  • The entities with the level 2 generation are the children of the entity with the level 1 generation and the parents of the entities with the level 3 generation.

  • And so on.

 

Entities are by default contained in a main hierarchy, but can also be present within alternative hierarchies. To know more on hierarchies, see its definition.

Entity type

Data collection point or consolidation node that can be attached to one or more indicators. It contains a set of entities for which data will be collected by default for the indicator.

 

Allows you to quickly associate an indicator with all entities with the same data collection level when creating a data collection campaign.

 

Also allows you to group entities together and prioritize them. For example: countries, regional headquarters, factories, etc.

Estimation (extrapolation method)

For campaigns of any type of periodicity.

The indicator value is estimated. In other words, the user does not have a real value for an indicator, but can estimate this value. The value is entered but identified as estimated.

F

  • Forecast

  • Framework

  • Functional scope

Forecast

Make it possible to predict the value of an indicator over the next periods of the current year as well as future years, based on the values already collected for this indicator and the projection calculation method.

Framework

Refers to a categorization of indicators according to a voluntary or mandatory standard, ratings or any other reference framework for categorizing indicators.

Functional scope

Users view indicators based on their assigned functional scope. For example, an HR user will only see social indicators.

H

  • Hierarchies

  • Historical data recovery

Hierarchies

Allow entities to be presented according to different visions: a main hierarchy and alternative hierarchies.

For example, if the main hierarchy has a legal vision, alternative hierarchies can provide an operational vision, by activity, by geographical area, and so on.

Historical data recovery

Integration of data from previous reporting periods into the CSR Insight application. This step allows you to keep track of historical data within the application.

I

  • Indicator

  • Inflation

  • Initiative

Indicator

Data collection and/or calculation is performed on indicators within campaigns. Each indicator can be deployed across one or more entities and periods. Data consolidation is performed across entities (entity aggregation) and periods (temporal aggregation).

 

An indicator can have the following response types:

  • Numerical data

  • Text

  • File

  • Choice list - simple choice

  • Choice list - multiple selection

Inflation

Inflation rate between two years set up for each entity. Applies to monetary data only.

Example: The year selected in the POV is 2026. Therefore, the inflation rate set up in 2026 will be applied on 2026 data.

Initiative

A good idea or practice that an organization can implement to achieve its goals. An initiative can be transformed into action.

M

  • Management

Management

Term used in the name of certain administration screens: Entity Management, Indicator Management, Emission Factor Management, etc.

Means that items can be defined, modified, deleted, in the screen.

P

  • Previous period (extrapolation method)

  • Previous year (extrapolation method)

  • Profile

  • Proforma

Previous period (extrapolation method)

Only for half-yearly, quarterly and monthly campaigns.

The indicator value is calculated based on the last period value.

Previous year (extrapolation method)

For yearly campaigns only.

The previous year's value is copied to the indicator.

Profile

Refers to a set of rights granted to users, in terms of organization, functional scope, and strategies.

 

Typical profiles are:

  • Administrator

  • Contributor

  • Reviewer

  • And so on

Proforma

A proforma records all changes related to past data over time.

 

By calculating different items that explain the variation between two periods, the Proforma feature distinguishes the induced performance of your company from the impacts of exogenous items. The Proforma feature restores comparability.

 

In the CSR Insight application, the Proforma feature is connected to the Carbon footprint module. Proforma is first used to compare your published carbon footprint data with your proforma data. You can also compare data from different proforma versions.

S

  • Same period of last year (extrapolation method)

  • Section

  • Stake

  • Strategy

  • Strategy deployment

Same period of last year (extrapolation method)

Only for half-yearly, quarterly and monthly campaigns.

The indicator value is calculated based on the last year value at the same period.

Section

Third classification level for indicators. Indicators defined in the CSR Insight application are divided into chapters, themes, and sections, by nesting:

  • Each chapter contains one or multiple themes.

  • Each theme contains one or multiple sections.

  • Each section contains one or multiple indicators.

Stake

First level of strategy deployment.

For example, a company implements a strategy to improve its CSR performance. As part of this strategy, the company will consider various stakes, such as environmental, social, and governance stakes.

Strategy

Item of CSR Insight that allows you to define and monitor an organization's strategies such as the decarbonization plan.

Strategy deployment

Consists of developing a strategy on different levels (stakes, targets level 1, targets level 2 and actions).

T

  • Target

  • Theme (Administration module)

  • Theme (Strategy module)

  • Threshold

Target

Second and third levels of strategy deployment. There are two levels of targets: level 1 and level 2.

For example, a company implements a strategy to improve its CSR performance. As part of this strategy, the company will consider various stakes, such as environmental, social, and governance stakes. For each stakes, the company will set targets. For the environmental stake, the company will set two targets level 1: one to reduce air pollution, the other to reduce water pollution. For each target, one or more targets level 2 are set: reducing GHG emissions and reducing the consumption of plant protection products.

Theme (Administration module)

Second classification level for indicators. Indicators defined in the CSR Insight application are divided into chapters, themes, and sections, by nesting:

  • Each chapter contains one or multiple themes.

  • Each theme contains one or multiple sections.

  • Each section contains one or multiple indicators.

Theme (Strategy module)

Make it possible to sort actions. Each theme can be linked to one or more actions.

 

For example, the theme "Energy Consumption" can be linked to the actions "Pool the budgets of the parent company and its subsidiaries," "Purchase premises compatible with the installation of solar panels," or "Reduce the number of computers used within the company."

 

Example 2: The theme "Social" can be linked to the actions "Raise employee awareness of sustainable development through internal training," "Provide employees with a relaxation area," or "Organize events that promote team building."

Threshold

Consists of defining a constant value to be respected for a numeric indicator: for example, less than or greater than 50, 100, between 0 and 1, and so on.

 

There are four types of thresholds:

  • Maximum

  • Strict maximum

  • Minimum

  • Strict minimum

 

Thresholds can be combined to create ranges of values ​​to be respected: for example, between 0 and 10.

U

  • Uncertainty

  • Unit

  • User

Uncertainty

Used in the Carbon Footprint module, uncertainty refers to the potential gap between the measured greenhouse gas emissions value (the value entered for an activity indicator or emission factor) and the CO2e actually emitted. Uncertainty is measured as a percentage.

Unit

In the CSR Insight application, you can use three types of units:

  • Default unit

  • Input unit: the unit of data entered for the indicator

  • Preference unit: the unit to which your entered data will be converted

 

For example, the default unit for the "Quantity of water consumed" indicator is liters (L). If you enter a value on the Data Collection Campaign screen for this indicator in a value other than the default, namely kiloliters (kL). This is the input unit. If you want to convert your entered value to milliliters (mL), this is the preference unit.

User

Person who uses the application and for whom you can attach one or more profiles.

V

  • Valuation table

  • Variation

Valuation table

Contains a coefficient that allows you to modify the weight of the activity indicator. This coefficient is included in the emissions calculation formula, as well as in classical reportings.

Variation

Refers to a difference between two numerical values ​​from one period to another.

W

  • Weighting (extrapolation method)

  • Weight of a reference indicator over a period of your choice (extrapolation method)

  • Workflow

Weighting (extrapolation method)

For yearly campaigns only.

The indicator value is calculated based on the weight of a reference indicator over a chosen year.

Weight of a reference indicator over a period of your choice (extrapolation method)

Only for half-yearly, quarterly and monthly campaigns.

The indicator value is calculated based on the weight of a reference indicator over a chosen period.

Workflow

Process containing multiple steps associated with statuses.

 

It allows you to add validation levels to data. A workflow step can be validated by a different user than the previous one. For example, a contributor can enter data, but only a reviewer can validate the data.

 

In the CSR Insight application, there are two types of workflows: one applied to indicators, the other to campaigns.
