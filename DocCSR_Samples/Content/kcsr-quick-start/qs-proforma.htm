Introduction

A proforma records all changes to historical data over time.

By calculating different items that explain the variation between two periods, the Proforma feature distinguishes the induced performance of your company from the impacts of exogenous items. The Proforma feature restores comparability.

In the CSR Insight application, the Proforma feature is connected to the Carbon footprint module. Proforma is first used to compare your published carbon footprint data with your proforma data. You can also compare data from different proforma versions.

NOTE To know more on carbon footprint, see the Carbon Footprint documentation.

This documentation has three goals:

  • Make you Understand the Key Concepts of the Proforma

  • Explain briefly the steps to follow in the CSR Insight application to Carry Out your Proforma

  • Indicate where you can View the Reporting of your Proforma Data

Understand the Key Concepts of the Proforma

In this section, you can learn more on the key concepts of the proforma:

  • Proforma Principles

  • Reference Version

  • Restatement

Proforma Principles

The principles of how the proforma works are:

  • A proforma is created with a start year, an end year and intermediate limits.

  • Changes are reported on the start year and the intermediate limits.

  • The end year is considered as a reference year, and so is not impacted by the changes.

Reference Version

A reference version corresponds to a set of baseline data used to calculate restatements within a proforma version.

By default, the reference version is the Published (data from a published carbon footprint).

After creating your first proforma version, you can select it as the reference version for subsequent versions.

Restatement

A restatement or proforma effect or correction (the three terms are used in the CSR Insight application) corresponds to a modification of past data from one year to another.

There are five types of restatements:

  • Emission factor effect

  • Inflation effect

  • Amortization effect

  • Correction of data collection

  • Scope variation

Each restatement type can be carried out in a specific screen in the CSR Insight application.

To know more about the restatement settings, see the Carry Out your Proforma section just below.

Carry Out your Proforma

NOTE The reference version of your first proforma for a given year must be a published carbon footprint. Therefore, to carry out your first proforma, you must have previously published a carbon footprint.

This prerequisite does not apply to the subsequent proforma. For these proforma, the reference version can be either another proforma or a published carbon footprint.

To carry out your proforma, you must follow several steps in the CSR Insight application:

  1. Create a proforma on the Proforma Version Management screen.

  2. Optional: To help you set up your proforma, you can view the variation in the emission factor value between two years on the Variation of Emission Factors screen.

  3. Set up the restatements:

    • Emission factor effect: To change an emission factor value or to associate an emission factor to another activity indicator, go to the Configuration of the Effect of Emission Factors screen.

    • Inflation effect: To define an inflation rate between the current year selected in the point of view (POV) and the previous year, go to the Configuration of the Inflation Effect screen.

    • Amortization effect: To change the amortization duration, go to the Configuration of the Amortization Effect screen.

    • Correction of data collection: To correct an indicator value entered on the Data Collection Campaigns screen, or to add an nonexistent value, go to the Correction of data collection screen.

    • Scope variation: To make changes in the organizational scope, that is to enter or exist entities from the scope, go to the Entity Management screen.

    WARNING To ensure that your proforma data is correctly updated in the reporting screens, click the button present in each setting screen after each restatement.

    This recommendation does not apply for the entries or exits of entities from the scope.

    Your proforma is set up.

You can now view the reporting of your proforma data. See the View the Reporting of your Proforma Data section just below.

View the Reporting of your Proforma Data

When your proforma data is well set up, you can view the data reporting.

The Proforma Reporting for One Year screen gives you a summary of all the restatements and their impacts for each proforma.

With the Proforma Reporting screen, you can view a summary of all the restatements and their impacts in a graphical or table form for each proforma. You can also select only one or several restatements to obtain a more focused summary.

Moreover, with the Multiyear Evolutions screen, you can view the evolution of the reference version data and proforma data between several years in a graphical or table form, and make comparisons.

NOTE On several non-proforma reporting screens, you can select the data source, that is the Published or a proforma version.
