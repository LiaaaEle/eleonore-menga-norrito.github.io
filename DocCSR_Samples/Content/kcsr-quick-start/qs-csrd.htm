Introduction

NOTE This documentation is aimed at companies that must comply with the CSRD standard.

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, also called CSRD, is a European Union standard that requires large companies and listed SME companies to publish regular sustainability reports on the social and environmental risks they face, and on how their activities impact people and the environment (dual materiality analysis).

The Regulatory and voluntary reporting feature makes it possible to map between the items required by the CSRD standard and the items available in the CSR Insight application, then generate your sustainability report.

 

The CSR Insight items concerned are:

  • Indicators

  • Emission posts

  • Emissions, also called emission sub-categories in the CSR Insight application

  • Scopes

  • Stakes, targets level 1, targets level 2, and/or actions

 

This documentation has two goals:

  • Make you Understand the Mapping Key Concepts for the CSRD Standard

  • Explain briefly the steps to follow in the CSR Insight application to Carry Out your Mapping and Generate a Report

Hierarchy of CSRD Standard Items

NOTE Not all ESRS are mandatory. Companies should only follow relevant items and information identified during the dual materiality selection phase.

The CSRD standard is based on European Sustainability Reporting Standards, also called ESRS. These standards are hierarchized as follows:

  • Cross-cutting standards:

    • ESRS 1 on General requirements

    • ESRS 2 on General disclosures

  • Thematic standards:

    • Environmental component:

      • ESRS E1 on Climate change

      • ESRS E2 on Pollution

      • ESRS E3 on Water and marine resources

      • ESRS E4 on Biodiversity and ecosystems

      • ESRS E5 on Resource use and circular economy

    • Social component:

      • ESRS S1 on Own workforce

      • ESRS S2 on Workers in the value chain

      • ESRS S3 on Affected communities

      • ESRS S4 on Consumers and end-users

    • Governance component:

      • ESRS G1 on Business conduct

Each ESRS meets Disclosure requirements (DR).

ESRS 2 contains MDRs, meaning Minimal disclosure requirements. MDRs mandates that all companies align their policies, actions, metrics, and targets. There are three categories of MDRs:

  • MDR Policies

  • MDR Action

  • MDR Target

Information and Mapping Levels in the CSR Insight Application

In the CSR Insight application, there are two or three levels of information between CSRD standard and CSR Insight items:

  • First level: Disclosure requirement (DR)

  • Second level: Data point (DP)

    NOTE An ESRS contains multiple data points.

  • Third level: Data part

    NOTE A data point can contain several information parts. A data part corresponds to an information part.

Mapping between CSRD standard and CSR Insight items is carried out at the data point or data part level.

Carry Out your Mapping and Generate a Report

To carry out your mapping, then generate a report:

  1. Go to the Generation of Regulatory Reports screen.

  2. Add, update, and delete links between CSRD standard and CSR Insight item as desired.

    A specific procedure is used to link stakes, targets level 1, targets level 2, or actions, to CSRD standard items:

    1. When creating or editing a stake, target level 1, target level 2, or action through the Strategy Deployment or Action Reports screen, link one of these items to one or multiple ESRS and enter summaries for each MDR that item contributes to.

    2. Edit or enter new summaries through the Summary of Contributions to MDRs screen.

      A link is automatically created between the CSR Insight item and the corresponding data points and data parts on the Generation of Regulatory Reports screen, except if they are already linked to another item type.

      This type of link is called Strategy.

  3. Generate a report in Word format.
